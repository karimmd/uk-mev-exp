# Provenance statement

This statement accompanies *Who Audits the MEV Detectors? Evidence-Based Adjudication of MEV Detections on Ethereum* and its supplementary document. It records how the adjudication was pre-specified, amended and revised, stated exactly rather than summarized. The registration files and their SHA-256 hashes, with their timestamps, are released beside it.

The analysis was pre-specified, with disclosed pre-analysis amendments and registered post-analysis revisions. It was independently implemented but not certifiably blinded, and the following are disclosed rather than summarized.

- **Accesses to the set-assignment map:** Three pre-analysis byte-level accesses occurred: a whole-file read by the verifying party at the first gate, a header read of two column names and zero data rows by the primary executor, and a digest computed by the verifying party at a later gate to check the file was unmodified. The third was supplied by that party against itself during audit, having gone unrecorded until then. A whole-file digest is provably label-free, and it is still an access, recorded under the same standard as the other two.
- **Unblinding:** No claim about unblinding is made. Whether unblinding occurred is a question about an agent's internal state that no artefact establishes. What is artefact-level is an armed seal, a scan of the hashed import graph, zero set or outcome tokens across all 17,637 output rows, a full independent recomputation of every outcome from the written rules with zero mismatches, and an unchanged output hash.
- **Artefacts written after the run:** Three hash-pinned artefacts were written after the run for comment-only or derived reasons. One is reconstructed and hash-verified against its recorded at-run value; two are not reconstructible. None is executable and none changed a case outcome.
- **An analysis version revised in place:** One earlier version of the analysis was revised in place rather than preserved separately. An independent before-and-after table is the preserved record.
- **Ordering of two plan amendments:** The ordering is not provable from the artefacts. It is attested and partially corroborated: the set map was independently verified unmodified before any outcome-linked join had occurred, and the results were produced by the final plan at its verified hash. No stronger statement about either party's state of knowledge is made anywhere in this work.
- **Explorer validation readings:** The first reading of the 459 explorer cards was made by claude-sonnet-5-5 through a headless coding session with tools disabled, under protocol version 1; its disagreements with the original witness located the W2 implementation error. The three-reader validation reported in the manuscript was then planned, with the readers, settings, verdict rule, no-answer rule and reported statistics hashed before the run (claude-opus-5 was tried first and excluded because its safety classifier declined every smoke-test card). The first three-reader run under version 1 located two wording defects in the protocol: its first question presupposed a buy-first bracket, and its profit field did not state whether it was before or after gas. Amendment 1 (protocol version 2) changes those two things and the output limit, and nothing else; it was hashed before the rerun. The version 2 readings are the ones reported; the version 1 readings are kept and reported beside them in the supplementary document.
- **Rules registered after pre-specification:** Three rules were registered after pre-specification. N2 was registered after a descriptive look at the sign and size of the residual positions; S5' after the three-way comparison, with the coverage under it seen before registration; W2 after the LLM validation located the implementation error it corrects. Each is a hashed file whose runner refuses to execute if the hash differs, and the pre-revision result is reported beside each.

Where timing has to be described, the accurate form is *before the legitimate outcome-linked join*. The analysis is not described as an unqualified independently blinded pre-registration, and the verifying party is not described as certifiably blinded.
